F.A.Q

Questions we get asked

What does Meridian Carriers do?

We are a freight forwarder and licensed customs broker. We arrange air, ocean and overland movement, clear the goods, and hold the warehousing and final-mile legs on the same file.

What industries do you specialise in?

Industrial equipment, automotive parts, FMCG, renewables and project infrastructure. Those lanes carry the compliance and out-of-gauge work our brokerage and project teams are built around.

What shipping methods do you offer?

Air consolidation and charter, ocean FCL, LCL, breakbulk and RoRo, plus domestic linehaul, wharf cartage and final mile.

Do you provide customs clearance services?

Yes — in-house, with licensed brokers. Entry lodgement, classification, valuation, duty concessions and audit support sit with the same team handling the shipment.

Can you handle oversized or project cargo?

Yes. Out-of-gauge and heavy-lift moves are surveyed, routed and permitted as a single engineered scope, including lift plans and site delivery.

How do I get a quote?

Send the lane, commodity, volume and target dates. You will get a line-by-line rate rather than a single blended number, so every charge is visible before you commit.

Ready to move smarter?

Tell us the lane. We will tell you exactly what it costs and exactly when it lands.
